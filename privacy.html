Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What the App accesses

When you authorize ClipFarm Uploader with TikTok, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

How it is used

This data is used only to upload videos you have approved to your own account and to show the result of each upload. Tokens are stored locally on your own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the App's local data folder removes all stored tokens and data.

Contact

For questions, contact the app owner through the TikTok account that authorized the App.
